Record yourself

Listening back is the fastest way to hear uneven rhythm and rushed passages. Takes stay on this device until you close the page.

Songs

Public-domain pieces arranged from first steps to early intermediate. Start in Wait mode (the music waits for you), then switch to Play mode to be scored on timing. Slow it down — 60% is a perfectly good tempo.

Technique

Scales, arpeggios, five-finger patterns, Hanon, chords and accompaniment patterns, with fingering on every key. Rule of thumb: three clean runs in a row, then raise the tempo by 4 BPM.

Rhythm

Timing is what makes playing sound musical. Tap with Space, the pad below, any key on your MIDI piano — or the mic picks up your taps on the piano.

Note reading

Name the note on the staff — play it on your piano, click the keyboard, or pick the letter. Notes you miss come back more often.
